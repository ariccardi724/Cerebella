CEREBELLA: FIND YOUR BALANCE - UPLOAD TO NETLIFY

1. Go to app.netlify.com/drop and sign in to your free account (so the site is kept).
2. Drag this ZIP file onto the page. (If you see "Page not found" afterwards,
   unzip it and drag the unzipped folder instead.)
3. When it says the site is published, copy the https://...netlify.app link.
   Optional: in Site configuration > Change site name, pick a friendlier name,
   for example cerebella-yourclinic.netlify.app.

ON THE iPad
4. Open the link in Safari.
5. Share > Add to Home Screen > Add (leave "Open as Web App" on if shown).
6. Open Cerebella from the Home Screen icon once on Wi-Fi. After that it works offline.

ON A META QUEST HEADSET (VR)
7. Put on the headset, open Meta Quest Browser, and go to the same link.
8. Start a session. On gaze and eye exercises, tap "Enter VR" in the top banner.
   Sit in a sturdy chair. Trigger or pinch pauses; the grip button leaves VR.
   Balance exercises are always done without the headset.

UPDATING LATER
Open your site's Deploys page on Netlify and drag the new zip or folder onto it.
The link stays the same; iPads update the next time Cerebella is opened online.

Profiles and sessions are stored on each device only and are not uploaded anywhere.
